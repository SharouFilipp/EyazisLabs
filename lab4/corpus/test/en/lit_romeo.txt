Romeo and Juliet by William Shakespeare

Romeo and Juliet is a tragedy by William Shakespeare. The action takes place in Verona, in Italy. Two noble families, the Montagues and the Capulets, hate each other.

Romeo, a young Montague, meets Juliet at a ball. The two young people fall in love immediately, although their families are enemies. They marry secretly with the help of a monk.

Soon a fight breaks out in the street. Romeo kills Tybalt, the cousin of Juliet, and the prince exiles him from the city. Juliet drinks a potion that makes her seem dead. Romeo does not know the truth. He finds her in the tomb and takes poison. When Juliet wakes up, she sees him dead and kills herself.

The play shows the power of love and the absurdity of hatred. The death of the lovers finally reconciles the two families.

The story of Romeo and Juliet inspired many operas, ballets and films. Today the two lovers remain a universal symbol of passion.
