The Little Prince by Antoine de Saint-Exupery

The Little Prince is a short novel by Antoine de Saint-Exupery. The book was published in 1943. It is one of the most translated books in the world.

The narrator is a pilot who has an accident in the desert. There he meets a strange little boy who comes from another planet. The little prince tells him about his journey and about the people that he met.

On each planet, the prince visits an adult: a king, a businessman, a geographer. These characters seem ridiculous, because they only think about power, money or numbers. The adults do not see what is essential.

On Earth, the prince meets a fox. The fox teaches him the meaning of friendship. He explains that the prince is responsible for his rose, because he loves her.

The book seems simple, but it raises deep questions about love, death and childhood. Children and adults read it with the same pleasure.
