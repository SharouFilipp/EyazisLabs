Hamlet by William Shakespeare

Hamlet is a tragedy by William Shakespeare. The play takes place in Denmark. The young prince Hamlet learns that his uncle killed his father. The uncle married the queen and became the new king.

The ghost of the father asks Hamlet to avenge his death. However, the prince hesitates for a long time. He thinks about life, death and the meaning of human actions. His famous monologue expresses his doubts.

Hamlet pretends to be mad. This madness allows him to observe the other characters. The young Ophelia loves Hamlet, but his strange behavior destroys her. She loses her mind and dies.

At the end of the play, almost all the main characters die. The tragedy shows that revenge leads to destruction. The hero is intelligent and sensitive, but he cannot act in time.

The play explores many important themes: power, betrayal, guilt and the conflict between thought and action. For this reason, Hamlet remains one of the most studied works in the world.
